# Startup & Founder Notes

A personal collection of notes, lessons, and synthesis on building startups, developing products, and thinking like a founder.

This repository captures ideas and insights from founders, entrepreneurs, investors, books, interviews, podcasts, lectures, and long-form conversations.

The purpose is not to create simple summaries or transcripts. Each note aims to extract the most important ideas, explain them clearly, and connect them with the realities of building companies in a rapidly changing technology landscape.

## Why I Keep These Notes

There is an enormous amount of startup advice available, but individual ideas are often consumed in isolation.

My objective is to go beyond collecting quotes or summarising conversations.

For each source, I try to understand:

1. **What is the core idea?**
2. **Why does it matter?**
3. **What assumptions are behind it?**
4. **How does it connect with other founder and startup lessons?**
5. **How could it be applied when building a real company?**

The goal is to move from:

> **Ideas → Understanding → Synthesis → Application**

## AI Startups

A particular area of interest is how Artificial Intelligence is changing the process of building startups.

AI is rapidly reducing the cost and time required to build software. As product development becomes faster, other capabilities become increasingly important:

- Choosing the right problem
- Understanding customers deeply
- Developing domain expertise
- Testing assumptions quickly
- Designing sustainable business models
- Building distribution
- Creating defensible advantages
- Developing proprietary data and learning loops
- Moving from impressive demos to reliable production systems
- Measuring outcomes rather than features

One question I want to explore through these notes is:

> **If building software is becoming easier, what will differentiate great founders and enduring companies?**
